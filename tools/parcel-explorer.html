    Snapshot year2025

    Each year TRPA snapshots every parcel in the Lake Tahoe Region with its development, zoning, and land use attributes. Stacked together, those snapshots let you follow one parcel, or thousands, across the years of the 2012 Regional Plan.

    Data

    • Parcel Development History (Cumulative Accounting service): one polygon per parcel per year, 2012 to present, with residential units, tourist accommodation units, commercial floor area, regional land use, plan and zoning, and town center location.
    • Tahoe APN Genealogy: splits, merges, and renumbering events that link old and new parcel numbers.
    • Parcels: the live parcel layer, used for address search and the current assessor record (assessed and taxable values, year built, ownership, existing land use).

    Notes

    • El Dorado County renumbered every parcel in 2018, adding a leading zero to the last segment (023-141-11 became 023-141-011). This tool joins the two formats so a South Shore parcel reads as one record.
    • The history layer carries no assessed values, addresses, or year built for any year, so those come from the current Parcels layer only.
    • Change tables are built in the browser from two full snapshots. A region-wide comparison loads about 120,000 records and takes 10 to 20 seconds.
    • A parcel that appears in only one of the two years is flagged New or Retired. Most of these are splits, merges, and renumbering rather than development.
