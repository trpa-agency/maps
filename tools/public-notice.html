Notice list

Builds the owner mailing list for a public notice: the parcels within a set distance of a subject property, with the owner mailing address on file at the county assessor.

  1. Subject property. Search an address or APN, click parcels with the arrow tool, or draw a point, line, or area with the pencil.
  2. Notice distance. Pick a preset or type a distance. The buffer is drawn on the map, and Find parcels lists everything it touches.
  3. Review. Untick rows, or use the orange arrow to add and remove parcels on the map. Owners of several parcels get one label when de-duplication is on.
  4. Export. CSV for the file, a PDF of Avery labels for the mailing, and a PNG of the map for the staff report.

Data

  • Parcels (TRPA GIS): the live parcel layer with situs and owner mailing addresses from the five county assessors.

Notes

  • Owner names are not published in the parcel service, so labels are addressed to the owner of record by role.
  • Roughly one parcel in a hundred has no usable mailing address on file (most are public agency holdings). Those rows stay in the table and the CSV flagged Incomplete, and are left out of the labels.
  • Owner-occupied means the mailing address matches the property address. With the occupant option on, those parcels get one label, not two.
  • Distances are measured on the ground (geodesic) from the subject parcel boundary.
