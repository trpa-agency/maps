1 Locations

Search an address, APN, EIP project, or “lat, lon” and pick a result. Up to 9 locations; each gets a numbered pin.

2 Style & layout

Style sets the logo and colors on the export. Layout sets what the export shows and its page shape.

3 Detail view

These options set the close-up map of the Detail + Basin and Detail Only layouts. Pick one of those in step 2 to use them.

The close-up map is what you see on screen: pan and zoom it, pick its basemap, and draw on it with the pencil tool (top right of the map).

Add to map

Branded EIP or TRPA maps of where a project is, ready to drop into a slide or report. Three steps:

  1. Add locations. Search an address, APN, EIP project, or “lat, lon” and pick a result. Up to 9; each gets a numbered pin.
  2. Choose a style and a layout. Style sets the logo and colors. Layout sets what the export shows:
  1. Save or Copy. Save downloads a PNG, a PowerPoint slide, or an SVG; Copy puts the image on the clipboard to paste straight into a slide. The detail layouts also let you pick the basemap, an imagery year (Nearmap 2021–2026, TRPA aerials from 1969 and 1940), and the zoom, and draw on the map with the pencil tool.

Each control in the panel explains what it does. Parcel data: TRPA GIS. The EIP Projects Map (link in the header) can send a selection of projects here with its Locator map button.
