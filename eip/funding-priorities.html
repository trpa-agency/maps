A shared workbench for deciding what gets funded next in the Environmental Improvement Program. It reads the same project records the EIP Project Tracker publishes, scores them against criteria you weight, tests a funding envelope, and collects panel scores from partner agencies.

  1. Candidate pool. Filter to the projects that compete: EIP projects in planning or implementation, by focus area, program, implementer, funding source, tag, state, funding status, and Lake Tahoe Restoration Act (LTRA) involvement.
  2. Funding envelope. Enter a dollar amount. Projects are funded in rank order by unfunded need (estimated cost minus secured funding, as the implementer reported it to the Tracker). Where secured funding is not reported, cost minus expenditures stands in and the row is marked with an asterisk. Pinned projects go first; excluded projects leave the pool.
  3. Priority projects. Each reviewer ticks up to ten projects, ranks them, adds notes, and clicks Send my picks, which opens a Survey123 form already filled in. Submissions land in TRPA's ArcGIS Online; the tool reads everyone's latest picks back (without email addresses) and combines them by Borda count: rank 1 earns 10 points, rank 10 earns 1, summed across reviewers. The cap gives every agency the same influence whatever the length of its list. Votes counts reviewers, Points is the Borda total, and the Reviewer points criterion carries it into the composite score. CSV export and import remain as a fallback. Your own picks and notes are also kept in this browser.
  4. Scoring weights (optional). The list is ordered by a 0 to 100 score: the weighted average of the criteria below that have data for a project. The Balanced preset applies unless you change it. Criteria without data for a project are left out of its average, and the Data column shows how much of the active weight had data.

Data

  • EIP Project Tracker (Lake Tahoe Info), via the nightly snapshot in trpa-agency/EIP.

Caveats

  • Projects without an estimated total cost are ranked but marked Unpriced, and the envelope cannot count them. Turn off Unpriced under Funding status to see the envelope against priced projects alone.
  • LTRA ask is the targeted (not yet secured) amount from Lake Tahoe Restoration Act funding sources in a project's expected funding table on the Tracker. Targeted is the same across all sources. Only public tags are shown.
  • Unfunded need, secured funding, and cost are self-reported by implementers and are only as current as their last Tracker update. Projects whose secured funding equals their cost show zero need and are funded at no cost to the envelope.
  • The Tracker assigns each project one action priority for reporting, so multi-benefit projects may not match the action priority you pick.
  • This tool does not write to the Tracker. Export the ranked list and panel scores to carry results forward.
